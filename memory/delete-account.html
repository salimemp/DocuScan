Delete Your Account & Data

Submit a request to permanently delete your DocScan Pro account and associated data.

If you wish to delete your DocScan Pro account, your scanned documents, and all related personal data, you can submit a deletion request below. We will verify ownership of your email address and complete the deletion within 30 days, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

What will be deleted

  • Your account record (email, hashed password, display name)
  • All scanned documents, OCR text, and uploaded files
  • Document history, tags, and folders
  • Saved business cards and contact extractions
  • Voice command history (if any)
  • Feedback messages associated with your account
  • Authentication and session logs

What will be retained

  • Anonymized usage statistics (no longer linked to you)
  • Deletion-request audit records (for compliance, retained 12 months)
  • Data we are legally required to keep (e.g., transaction records, if any)
⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you only want to take a break, consider signing out instead — you can sign back in anytime.

Request data deletion

Alternative: Email us directly

You can also email support@docscanpro.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address used to register your account.

Timeline

  • Within 24 hours: You will receive a confirmation email acknowledging your request.
  • Within 7 days: Identity verification (we may ask you to confirm via email).
  • Within 30 days: All data deleted from our active production systems.
  • Within 90 days: Data removed from encrypted backups.
